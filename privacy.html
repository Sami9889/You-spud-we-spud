Privacy

Short version: submissions stay private, no public list, no weird data sharing.

What we store

Name, email, Slack ID, phone, project link, repo link, file size, description, shipping address, and any admin notes. We don’t sell or share this.

Who can see it

No one on the public web can see submissions. Only logged-in admins can access the review board. There isn’t a separate reviewer role yet; right now it’s all-or-nothing.

How long we keep it

Submissions stay until they’re manually deleted. The only auto-expiring data is a 1-hour rate-limit counter to stop spam.

Questions or removal

If you want your submission removed, or have any questions, DM @sami9889 in #ship on Hack Club Slack.

You Spud,
We Spud.

Everyone's shipping 500KB of JavaScript just to show some text on a page. Build something under 15KB — no frameworks, no React, no Tailwind — and Sami will mail you a potato from Australia.

Ship your spud → Read the rules

What is this?

This is a You Ship, We Ship challenge run by Sami from Hack Club Slack. The idea is simple — build something small, ship it publicly, and get something mailed to you from Australia.

Can't send a raw potato through customs but a flat packet of instant mashed potatoes plus a custom sticker is totally fine. And if you somehow fit your project under 5KB, there's Australian chicken salt in it for you too. Once you try it you'll understand why it's worth it.

No gimmicks. You build it, Sami ships it.

The rules

Keep it simple. Keep it small. Keep it vanilla.

FAQ

What counts toward the 15KB?

All your files — HTML, CSS, JS, images. External stuff loaded from CDNs (like Google Fonts) doesn't count, but keep the spirit of it. The point is to prove you don't need a million dependencies.

Does it have to be a website?

It needs to run in a browser — a site, game, tool, interactive thing. No native apps. Beyond that, build whatever you want.

Does it have to be potato themed?

No, but it earns bonus points. Sami likes potatoes.

How long does shipping take?

It's coming from Australia so a few weeks depending on your country. It will arrive.

You Spud,
We Spud.

Everyone's shipping 500KB of JavaScript just to show some text on a page. Build something under 15KB (no frameworks, no React, no Tailwind) and Sami will mail you a parcel from Australia.

Ship your spud → Read the rules

What is this?

This is a You Ship, We Ship challenge run by Sami from Hack Club Slack. The idea is simple, build something small, ship it publicly, and get a parcel mailed to you from Australia.

Can't send a raw potato through customs, but stickers, snacks, and a proper airmail postcard all clear just fine. Hit the smaller size tiers and the parcel gets fatter.

No gimmicks. You build it, Sami packs it, Australia Post does the rest.

FAQ

What counts toward the 15KB?

All your files: HTML, CSS, JS, images. External stuff loaded from CDNs (like Google Fonts) doesn't count, but keep the spirit of it. The point is to prove you don't need a million dependencies.

Does it have to be a website?

It needs to run in a browser: a site, game, tool, interactive thing. No native apps. Beyond that, build whatever you want.

Does it have to be potato themed?

No, but it earns bonus points. Sami likes potatoes.

Is the parcel really more than potatoes?

Yes. Potatoes only show up in Ultra Hard Mode. Everyone else gets stickers, a postcard, snacks, and whatever else fits in the envelope that week.

How long does shipping take?

It's coming from Australia so a few weeks depending on your country. It will arrive.

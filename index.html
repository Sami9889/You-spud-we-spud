// what is this

The internet got fat.
We're going on a diet.

This is a You Ship, We Ship challenge run by Sami from the Hack Club Slack community. The premise is dead simple: build something real, keep it tiny, ship it publicly, and we'll mail you something from the far corners of Australia.

Raw potato? Customs won't allow it. But a flat packet of instant mashed potatoes and a custom Orpheus-in-a-spud-suit sticker? That's legally mailable to most countries on Earth. And for the truly deranged — those who keep it under 5KB — we're adding a sealed packet of Australian chicken salt. It will change your life.

03

Must Be Live

Deploy it somewhere publicly accessible. GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Hack Club's own Nest — just make it reachable.

04

Share Source

Link your repo alongside your live site when you post in #ship. Open source is the vibe.

// faq

Questions?

What counts toward the 15KB?

Everything: your HTML file, any linked CSS files, any JS files, and any images. External fonts and external CDN scripts technically don't count toward the byte total, but keep it in the spirit of the challenge — minimal is the vibe.

Can I use external fonts or icons?

Yes — external resources loaded from CDNs don't count toward the file size limit. But keep the spirit of the challenge in mind. The point is to prove you don't need 200MB of node_modules to ship something great.

Does it have to be a website?

It has to be deliverable as a web thing — a site, game, tool, or interactive experience that runs in a browser. No native apps. But beyond that, the format is up to you.

Can it be potato themed?

It doesn't have to be, but it earns bonus points. Sami loves potatoes. The more potato energy, the better your odds of receiving extra appreciation (and possibly extra chicken salt).

How long does shipping take?

It's coming from Australia, so international shipping times vary. Expect a few weeks. But it will arrive, and it will be worth it.

What is Hack Club?

Hack Club is a global nonprofit network of student-run coding clubs and a Slack community for teenage hackers. Free to join. Full of people shipping cool things. hackclub.com is the place.
